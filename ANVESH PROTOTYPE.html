About

About Anvesh

Anvesh (“manak” = standard, “setu” = bridge) is a conceptual AI-powered platform that bridges the gap between natural-language procurement requirements and the rich body of Indian Standards published by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS).

Public procurement often requires officers to identify the correct technical standards for goods and services. This platform demonstrates how modern NLP — entity extraction, semantic search and explainable ranking — can make that process faster, more consistent and fully auditable, while keeping the human officer firmly in control of final decisions.

This website is a design prototype. All standards data, metrics and project records shown are realistic demonstration data. It does not represent an official Government of India system and claims no official integration with BIS or any government API.

AI recommendations are advisory and subject to authorized human verification.
